Stellar Stellar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Stellar Stellar is a personal tool used only by its owner (contact: clintoy164@gmail.com) to post the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts.

What is stored

  • The posts, captions and media the owner writes or uploads.
  • Login tokens for the accounts the owner connects (for TikTok: basic profile info and permission to upload videos to drafts). Tokens are kept encrypted and are never shown or shared.
  • The IDs and links of published posts.

How it is used

Only to publish the owner's posts when the owner asks. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data goes only to the platform being posted to (such as TikTok) and to the services that run Stellar Stellar (Supabase for storage, GitHub for scheduled runs, Cloudflare for hosting).

Removing data

The owner can disconnect an account or delete posts at any time. Disconnecting removes the stored token. Access can also be revoked from the platform's own settings.

See also the Terms of Service.
